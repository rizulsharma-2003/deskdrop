Installing DeskDrop
===================

1. Drag DeskDrop onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open DeskDrop from your Applications folder.

   macOS will say it can't verify the developer, because this build
   isn't notarized by Apple. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the
   message about DeskDrop, click "Open Anyway", and confirm.

   On macOS 14 Sonoma you can instead Control-click DeskDrop in
   Applications, choose "Open", then click "Open" again.

   You only need to do this once.

4. When DeskDrop asks to find devices on your local network,
   click "Allow". Without it, DeskDrop can't see colleagues.

Using DeskDrop
--------------
- Colleagues running DeskDrop on the same network appear as Macs
  side by side in the window.
- To send, drag files from Finder onto a colleague's Mac and let go.
- When someone sends you a file, a request appears in the top-right
  corner of your screen. Nothing is saved until you click Accept.
  Received files go to your Downloads folder.
- Closing the window, or quitting with Cmd-Q, keeps DeskDrop
  running in the menu bar so colleagues can still send you files
  and messages, and you still get notifications. To stop it, click
  the menu bar icon and choose "Quit DeskDrop Completely".
- Installing a newer version? Quit DeskDrop Completely first.

Shortcuts (no window needed)
----------------------------
- Hold to share: start dragging any file, then pause for a moment.
  Your colleagues appear around the cursor. Drop on one to send.
- Screen edges: right-click a colleague in DeskDrop and choose
  "Assign to Left Edge" or "Assign to Right Edge". Then drag a file
  and push it against that edge of your screen. A portal opens;
  let go to send.
- Change these in DeskDrop > Settings > Gestures.

Messages
--------
- Click "Messages" at the top of the DeskDrop window (or press Cmd-2).
  Click "Nearby" (Cmd-1) to go back to your colleagues' Macs.
- "Everyone nearby" is a group chat with every colleague running
  DeskDrop; you can also message one colleague directly.
- Your chat history is saved on your Mac. Messages to someone who
  is offline are delivered when they're back.
- Hover a message and click the smiley beside it to react, or
  click + for any emoji. Double-click a message to copy it.
  The smiley next to the message box opens emoji.
- Allow notifications when DeskDrop asks, so new messages show a
  banner while DeskDrop is in the background. You can reply right
  from the banner. (Turned them off? System Settings >
  Notifications > DeskDrop.)
- Under your last message you'll see Delivered, then Read once
  your colleague opens the chat (you can turn read receipts off).
- Files you send to or receive from a colleague show up in your
  chat with them. Accept a request right there, then click the file
  to preview it or double-click to open it.
- Pick your own sounds for files and messages in
  DeskDrop > Settings > Receiving > Sounds.

DeskDrop sends files directly between Macs over an encrypted
connection. No account, no cloud, no internet needed.
It is not Apple's AirDrop.
